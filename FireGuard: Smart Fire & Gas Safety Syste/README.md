🔥 FireGuard: Smart Fire & Gas Safety System

An IoT-based local safety system built with an ESP32 microcontroller that monitors smoke, gas, and room temperature in real-time, triggering instant local alerts and automated emergency responses during hazards.

🎯 Key Features
📌Dynamic Detection:** Continuously monitors smoke/gas levels using the MQ-2 sensor and room temperature using the DHT22 sensor.
📌Local Emergency Response: Instantly triggers an emergency buzzer and red warning light upon detecting hazardous gas or high temperature.
✅Automated Safety Control: Utilizes a servo motor mechanism to automatically shut off gas lines or valves immediately when a hazard is detected.

📍 Applications & Use Cases
📌Kitchens:** Preventing gas leaks and cooking fire accidents with automated valve shutoff.
📌Offices & Server Rooms:** Early fire hazard detection to protect critical equipment.
📌🪚Home Safety:** General room and indoor fire monitoring.
