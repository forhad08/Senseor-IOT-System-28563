||Smart Aqua: The Intelligent Watering Assistant||

Smart Aqua is an IoT system that monitors soil moisture in real time and displays its status on an LCD screen.

🎯 Key Features:
 Measures soil moisture levels dynamically.  
 Displays live soil condition (Wet/Dry) on screen.  
 Helps automate plant watering and prevent overwatering.  

📍 Where It's Used:
 Home & Office Gardens: Automatic pot watering.
 Greenhouses & Rooftops: Maintaining optimal soil moisture.
 Agriculture: Efficient water management for crops.
